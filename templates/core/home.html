Every weld on the job ends up as one line like this.

Years after the pipe is buried, someone will ask where a weld is, what went into it, and whether you can prove it. A quality hand keeps the record that answers all three. Tier 1 teaches you to read it, fill it in and keep it straight.

Tier 1 is the quality side of the job, taught by someone who's done it.

Who it's for

Welders, helpers, operators, laborers and foremen who already know what good work looks like and want to move to the quality side. You don't need a quality background, and you don't need a CWI to start.

It covers gas and hazardous liquid lines and their stations, under 49 CFR 192 and 195.

What you'll be able to do

  • Find where any requirement on the job comes from, and which document wins when two disagree
  • Read the drawings and check the built work against them
  • Trace pipe and fittings from the mill cert to the weld
  • Number and map every weld and keep the records that pass turnover
  • Check welding, NDE, coating, bolting and pressure testing against the approved procedure
  • Run a QC day, and know where your authority stops
