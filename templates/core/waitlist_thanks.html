Weld field sheet

The sheet for the firing line. Write down every weld in the order the line runs: the pipe and heat on each side, and the welder stencils the way they sit on the pipe. At the end of the day the welds and the pipe should match, and that count tells you if something's missing.

  • Page 1 The blank sheet
  • Page 2 How to fill it in. Print it on the back.
  • Pages 3 to 5 Two days on a mainline spread, with a tie-in, a repair, a pup and a cut-out
  • Pages 6 and 7 A station line, with spools, a valve, a field fit, a branch and a socket weld
