Where Tier 1 stops

Recognize and record

Tier 1 teaches you to spot a problem and get it on the record. It doesn't replace a CWI, API 1169, AMPP or NDE cert, and it won't teach you to accept work that needs one of them to sign. Every module says where that line is.

It covers gas and hazardous liquid lines and their stations, under 49 CFR 192 and 195. Where a requirement changes from job to job, you learn where to find it in the project documents.

Who built it

From the field

I started as a field inspector. I've held the weld log and walked the right-of-way at 5 AM. FDQ comes out of fifteen years on pipeline, LNG, compressor station and industrial jobs, watching which quality programs worked in the field and which ones lived in a binder.

Tier 1 is the course for the hand who already knows what good work looks like and wants the quality side next.
